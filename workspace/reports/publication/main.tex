\section{Independent execution, selection, and protection rules}

The wheel initializes an arbitrary new workspace without importing the original project or upstream repositories. Import paths were checked from /tmp. Python, PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib remain required; full Chapter 3 budgets use CUDA. The tested environment was Python 3.12.9, PyTorch 2.5.1+cu121, NumPy 2.0.1, SciPy 1.15.1, Matplotlib 3.10.1, and A100 40GB.

The independent virtual environment shared scientific libraries already installed on the server. A clean-operating-system or fully offline dependency installation was not verified. DeepXDE LGPL, Galerkin MIT, and ocpde\_dl provenance are retained. Standalone does not mean wholly original or uniformly licensed.

During formal training, the 317 numerical source files and source manifest were unchanged; budgets were not reduced and old results were not substituted. Original data, results, the frozen Chapter 2 directory, and readable directories totaled 9,550 files and 7,868,164,617 bytes and matched the pretraining baseline individually. Source and artifact manifests are distinct and are not substitutes for license provenance. English presentation changes are documented separately in v1.0.1; the original manifests continue to identify v1.0.0.

\textbf{Recovery protocol:} Chapters 2-3 provide checkpoint recovery. Chapters 4-5 skip completed tasks only after their respective audits. Restarting a training run is not exact resumption. Do not unconditionally restart the pipeline in a completed formal workspace or remove a lock still held by a running process.
